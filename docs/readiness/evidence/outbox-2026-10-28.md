## Tests

`tests/integration/outbox.test.ts`: 11 of 11, with a channel that fails on purpose.

- A failed send leaves the change it reports in place, and the email waits for its next attempt.
- Two dispatchers at once send an email once; it's then marked sent with its link cleared.
- A real password reset is queued, sent at once, and its link appears in the mailbox only.
- Attempts happen at the stated times and never early; after the fifth the row is failed, audited
  for staff without its wording, and nothing tries again or elsewhere.
- An expired email is dropped.
- "Email not delivered" names the person and the kind of email, never the address or wording; it
  can be closed; care guides don't see it.
- A nudge read in the app, or with email turned off, is dropped; quiet hours hold it without
  counting; different wording or a changed address is dropped and audited without content; the
  audit chain checks clean.
- The notice sender still sends right away when nothing stops it.

## Limits

- **A real channel could send one email twice in one case.** Each send is claimed first, so two
  dispatchers never send the same email. But a channel that accepts an email and then fails to
  answer would look like a failure, and the next attempt would send it again. Handling that (an
  idempotency key with the provider) belongs to the real channel's integration (D-06); the
  development mailbox can't fail this way.
- **"Email not delivered" needs a household** to show in the queue. Every current kind has one; an
  email about no household would be audited but not listed.
- **Addresses are kept** in the outbox after sending (who was emailed, and when) under the
  `notification` record class, marked for counsel review with retention (D-05).
