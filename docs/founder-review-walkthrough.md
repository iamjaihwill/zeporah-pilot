# Founder-review walkthrough

An internal walkthrough of the synthetic pilot prototype, about 41 minutes, plus about 12 for the operational screens at the end. Everything shown is synthetic. **Seeded** means it's already in the demo data; **Live** means you create it during the walkthrough. Live steps change the demo data, so press **Reset synthetic data** on the start page before you begin and again when you finish.

Switch personas with **Sign out** (top left), then pick the next person under **Development only: sign in as a synthetic persona** on the start page. Persona sign-in needs the development gate, which `bun run dev` (or the preview configuration `zeporah-pilot-dev-personas`) turns on. Personas have no passwords. Record observations in [founder-review-notes-template.md](founder-review-notes-template.md).

## What's seeded vs. live

- **Seeded:** Chidi's held consent; Ada's amended consent (version 2); Ravi's open correction (Patel); Ingrid's dismissed correction (Lindqvist); the unlinked Rivera authority record; Willow Creek's wording (2 versions); Cedar Valley's wording turned off; history for the Hart and Bennett escalations; Nguyen with no care guide (Tomás's assignment ended); Lindqvist's 13-month bereavement plan (first check-in due this week).
- **Live:** Grace's correction and the release hold (steps 5–7); optional confirmation by Ada (step 4); the bereavement transition (step 11); assigning and ending a care guide (step 12a). For the full invite → accept → assign journey, Dana can also invite a care guide from **Hospice settings** and pass on the one-time link.
- **Not yet demonstrable:** a household member without the Sharing page who holds an authority workflow and confirms through "Consents for you" — it needs a new persona, which hasn't been approved.
