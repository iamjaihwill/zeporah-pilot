# Threat model (draft, 2026-10-29)

**Status: a draft for PB-04. Not reviewed, not accepted, and not evidence that any threat is
handled.** PB-04 closes only after a review session with the privacy and security officer, and when
every high threat has a control or a signed acceptance. Ratings are the drafter's first estimate,
for the reviewer to change. Revision: `main` after `021d10e`. Companions:
[data-flow.md](data-flow.md) and [trust-boundaries.md](trust-boundaries.md).

**Scope.** The application as it is, and the planned hosted shape ([deployment.md](deployment.md))
where a threat only appears once it's hosted. Out of scope until their decisions: partner services
(Track B), employer contexts, live AI (R-01), live integrations (R-04).

**Assets, in order of harm if exposed or altered.** Protected household information (consents,
authority evidence, vault metadata, internal notes, messages); who is in a household and their
care stage; the audit and access records; staff and family credentials and sessions; the 24-hour
line families are shown; the hospice's configuration.

**Adversaries.** Someone outside with no account; a family member reaching beyond their own
household or role; a person pressuring a household member (coercion); a staff member reading or
changing more than their role allows; a released packet's recipient after their access ends; an
operator or developer with database or log access; a compromised dependency.

**Rating.** Likelihood and impact are Low, Medium or High for a 10–25-household pilot with real
data. **Status** is the control's state today: **controlled** (built, with an executed automated
test), **partial** (built in part, or tested only on the embedded database), **open** (no control).
"Controlled" here never means reviewed or approved.
